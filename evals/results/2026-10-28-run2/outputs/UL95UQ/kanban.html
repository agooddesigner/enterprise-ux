How to move tickets
  • Mouse or touch-pad: drag a ticket and drop it in a column.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a ticket's Reorder button, press Space or Enter to pick it up, use ← → to change column and ↑ ↓ to change position, then Space or Enter to drop. Esc cancels.
  • Any device, no dragging: choose a column in a ticket's Move to list and press Move.
