How to move tickets
  • Mouse or touch: drag a ticket by its title row and drop it in any column.
  • Keyboard or screen reader: focus a ticket's title button and press Space or Enter to pick it up. Left and Right arrows change column, Up and Down reorder, Home and End jump to top or bottom. Press Space or Enter to drop, or Escape to cancel.
  • No dragging: every ticket has a Move button that opens a dialog to choose a column.
