Support ticket board

Track support tickets from New to Done. Move a ticket by dragging its handle, with the keyboard, or with each ticket’s “Move…” button.

How to move tickets
  • Mouse, touch or pen: drag a ticket (on touch, drag by the grip handle) and drop it on a column.
  • Keyboard or screen reader: focus a ticket’s grip button, press Space to pick it up, ← → to change column, ↑ ↓ to reorder, Space or Enter to drop, Esc to cancel.
  • No dragging at all: use the “Move…” button on a ticket and choose a column.
